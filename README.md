# GRE Lexicon

**Live:** https://quan-nk.github.io/gre-lexicon/

A small study app for GRE Verbal, built to fit a full-time job: an hour at lunch, three hours at night, about 25 days to the test.

- **Words** (lunch): 20 words a day for 15 days. Each word has US pronunciation with audio, its meaning, and 5 example sentences. The last tab, *Test your vocab*, mixes the day's sentences so you recall the words in context.
- **Essays** (spare minutes): 200 model Issue essays at the 6 level. Each essay highlights its vocabulary and explains why it scores a 6, plus three moves worth borrowing.
- **Writing** (evenings): a timed essay, error-spotting drills and a proofreading toolkit. Feedback and grading are in development.

Jump straight to a section by adding `#d05`, `#d05-test`, `#essays` or `#write` to the link. Progress is saved in your browser.
